# Caesar's *De Bello Gallico* - Vocabulary Learning Progression

This curriculum organizes all Latin words in Caesar's *De Bello Gallico* by order of first appearance.
All conjugated verbs, nouns, and adjectives are reduced to their canonical dictionary headword;
once a word is introduced, all subsequent conjugated forms are eliminated from future study steps.

## 1. Book 1 Micro-Chunk Breakdown (~200–260 New Words per Step)

To make starting Caesar effortless, Book 1 is split into **6 bite-sized stages** of equal learning load.
