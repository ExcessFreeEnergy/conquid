# Caesar's *De Bello Gallico* - Complete 16-Stage Vocabulary Curriculum

This curriculum breaks down all 8 books of Julius Caesar's *De Bello Gallico* (**53,676 total words**) into **16 bite-sized learning stages**.
Books with over 300 new words (Books 1, 2, 5, and 7) are divided into natural narrative subchunks so that **every single step requires learning between 144 and 264 new words**.
All conjugated verbs, declined nouns, and adjectives are reduced to their canonical dictionary headwords; once a word is introduced, all subsequent conjugated forms are permanently removed from future stages.
